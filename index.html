Platform-X

Collector Growth Plan

How Platform-X works today

A quick picture of the app

Companies need information. For example, Kifiya in Addis Ababa wants to know which small shops accept mobile payments. It posts a task. People called collectors do the task on their phone. A checker looks at the answers. Good answers are paid in ETB. Collectors take the money out with Telebirr, Chapa or CBE.

1Company posts a taskIt sets the questions, the pay and the area.
2Collector does itOn the phone, even with no internet.
3Checker approvesGood work is accepted. Bad work is sent back.
4Collector gets paidETB goes into the app wallet.
5Collector cashes outTo Telebirr, Chapa or CBE.

The app already has a wallet, bonuses, invite codes, reminders and a timer for weekly jobs. This plan uses them. We do not change how tasks work.

The problem

Why Platform-X needs this

A task app only works if collectors keep coming back and do good work. Without a reason to stay, most of them drift away.

  • People try the app once and leave. A clear profile and level path shows new collectors how they can grow.
  • No task, no visit. Weekly targets give a steady reason to come back.
  • Pay alone does not keep people. Levels with real work benefits make collectors want to stay.
  • Invites bring the wrong people. Referral rewards come only after the friend does approved work.
  • Some places get no data. Coverage bounties pay more where data is missing.
  • Careless work hurts quality. Every reward depends on approved work and the Trust Score.

What we want

What we want to grow

  • More people join
  • Collectors keep working week after week
  • Good collectors invite good people
  • More good tasks get done
  • Find the best people to hire

The one rule

Every reward comes from good work

Companies pay us for true, good information. This plan supports that work. It is not a game added on top.

  • Rewards come only when work is approved.
  • Each collector has a Trust Score. Good work makes it go up. Bad work makes it go down.
  • A low Trust Score lowers bonuses and limits access to better tasks.
  • Nothing is random. There are no prizes based on luck.

Two words to know

Trust Score and levels

Trust Score

A score for the quality of your work. It is not money. It goes up with approved work and down with rejected work.

Levels

Work grades, like at a job. A good Trust Score and steady approved work move you up. Higher levels see better-paying tasks first.

The plan

4 strategies, in the order we should build them

Each one supports the core job: good, approved data work. Number 1 should be built first.

1

Collector Profile and Trust Score

More good work

Each collector gets a work profile: level, Trust Score, approved tasks and skills. Good work moves you to a higher level, and each level brings real work benefits.

Why first: everything else depends on it. It rewards quality, costs little, and builds the work history Kifiya needs.

What is inside

  • Trust Score: a score for the quality of your work. Approved work raises it. Rejected work lowers it.
  • Levels: work grades, like at a job. Higher levels see better-paying tasks first, get more task types and faster payout review.
  • Welcome task: a short practice task that teaches new collectors how to do good work.
  • Later, certificates: pass a short test to prove a skill, like photo surveys or shop checks, and get special tasks.
  • Later, small loans: a long, good work history can help collectors get small loans from Kifiya.

Already used by

  • Uber ProDrivers move up tiers by doing good work and get real benefits at each tier.
  • Field AgentA quality score decides who sees jobs. Rejected work lowers it.
  • Google Maps Local GuidesPeople who add facts about places move up levels.
  • TolokaSkill scores open better-paying tasks.
2

Weekly Targets and Consistency Bonus

Steady work

Each collector gets a clear weekly work target. Collectors who meet their target week after week earn a consistency bonus.

Why second: it keeps collectors working steadily, like a performance bonus at a normal job. It is easy to build on top of part 1.

How it works

  • Each Monday the app sets a weekly target, based on your past work and the tasks near you.
  • Only approved tasks count toward the target.
  • Meet the target several weeks in a row to earn a consistency bonus.
  • A grace rule: one missed week does not reset your progress.
  • A reminder when new tasks are posted near you.

Already used by

  • Uber and Bolt driver targetsDrivers get a bonus for finishing a set number of trips in a week.
  • Uber ProStatus is kept by doing steady, good work over time.
3

Referral and Team Program

More people joinGood invites

Collectors invite people they trust. Rewards come only when the new person does real, approved work. Small teams can also share a weekly target.

Why third: it grows the number of collectors with real workers, not fake sign-ups. It needs parts 1 and 2 to measure good work.

How it works

  • Referral rewards in steps: you are paid as your friend completes more approved tasks.
  • Optional small teams: a few collectors share one weekly target, set by the app. If they reach it, each member gets the same bonus.
  • Nobody pays money in. All bonuses come from the Platform-X budget.
  • Kifiya sets the rules and the money limit in the admin website.

Already used by

  • Field Agent and PremiseBoth pay collectors for inviting friends.
4

Coverage Bounties

More tasks doneHappy companies

A map shows where data is still needed. Tasks in those areas pay more, so collectors go where companies need them most.

Why fourth: it brings the most value to companies, but it is the hardest to build. Today the phone tells us where the collector is, and we trust it. We must add location checks on our side first.

Example

  • Kifiya needs data on small shops in Kolfe for a loan product. Nobody has gone there yet.
  • The app marks Kolfe as an area that needs data, with higher pay.
  • Collector-A sees it 2 km away and visits 5 shops. Each task pays 80 ETB, not 40.
  • Kifiya sees on its map that Kolfe is now covered.

Also part of this

  • Urgent tasks: when a company needs data fast, nearby collectors get an alert and higher pay.
  • For companies: add extra pay to make a task go faster, and see a map of where data is still missing.

Already used by

  • PremisePays people to do location tasks like taking photos of shops.
  • Uber surge mapA map shows drivers where they are needed and where pay is higher.
  • Field AgentPays a little more to do several tasks at one place.

Staying safe

Rules to protect money and trust

A money limitBonuses have a fixed ETB budget each month. When it runs out, no new bonuses are paid that month.
Watch the costWe check how much ETB the bonuses cost for each approved task.
No luckEvery reward is earned by work. Nobody can pay in.
Stop cheatingBonus limits, one account per phone, location checks, and referral rewards only after real work.
Fair for people with no internetWork counts on the day it was done, not the day it was sent.
PrivacyA collector's profile is seen only by the collector and Kifiya. No public rankings.

How we build it

One new part, nothing old breaks

We add one new part to the system that keeps the Trust Score, levels, targets and bonuses. It listens to what already happens in the app, like "task approved". When a bonus is earned, it asks the wallet to pay it. The way tasks work today does not change.

In the phone app

  • A "My Work" page: level, Trust Score, weekly target and certificates.
  • This week's target on the home screen.
  • A clear message when a bonus is paid.

In the admin websites

  • Kifiya team: set targets, bonus amounts and the money limit.
  • Companies: add extra pay to a task, and see the coverage map.

Extra value for Kifiya

Find the best people to hire

When Kifiya needs to hire, the work data shows who the best collectors are. No guessing from a CV alone.

  • Trust Score shows who does careful, honest work over a long time.
  • Levels and weekly targets show who is steady and keeps showing up.
  • Team leaders show who can lead and help others.
  • Certificates show who is good at special work, like photos or surveys.
  • Coverage work shows who is ready to travel to new areas.
