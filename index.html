A game plan for Platform-X

Platform-X Quest

How Platform-X works today

A quick picture of the app

Companies need information. For example, Kifiya in Addis Ababa wants to know which small shops accept mobile payments. It posts a task. People called collectors do the task on their phone. A checker looks at the answers. Good answers are paid in ETB. Collectors take the money out with Telebirr, Chapa or CBE.

1Company posts a taskIt sets the questions, the pay and the area.
2Collector does itOn the phone, even with no internet.
3Checker approvesGood work is accepted. Bad work is sent back.
4Collector gets paidETB goes into the app wallet.
5Collector cashes outTo Telebirr, Chapa or CBE.

The app already has a wallet, bonuses, invite codes, reminders and a timer for daily jobs. The game can use all of these. We do not need to change how tasks work.

The problem

Why Platform-X needs this

A task app only works if collectors keep coming back and do good work. Without a reason to stay, most of them drift away.

  • People try the app once and leave. Points, levels and a welcome mission give them a reason to stay.
  • No task, no visit. Daily goals and days in a row bring people back every day.
  • Pay alone does not keep people. Levels, badges and teams make them feel part of Platform-X.
  • Invites bring the wrong people. Invite rewards come only after the friend does good tasks.
  • Some places get no data. The city map pays more where data is missing.
  • Careless work hurts quality. Points come only from approved work.

What we want

What we want to grow

  • More people join
  • People come back daily
  • People invite friends
  • More tasks get done
  • Find the best people to hire

The one rule

Only good work gets points

Companies pay us for true, good information. If we give points for speed, people will work carelessly or cheat. So the game has one rule.

  • You get points only when your work is approved.
  • Each collector has a Trust Score. Good work makes it go up. Bad work makes it go down.
  • A high Trust Score gives more points. A low one gives fewer.
  • If your work is rejected, you lose some points.

Two words to know

Points and ranks

Points (XP)

Points are a score for good work. They are not money and you cannot cash them out. They show how far you have come.

Example: Collector-A gets 5 tasks approved. She gets 200 ETB in her wallet and 100 points.

Ranks

When you collect enough points, you move up a level, like a belt in karate. Higher levels see better-paying tasks first.

Levels

The 6 levels

Kifiya Starter -> Kifiya Explorer -> Kifiya Achiever -> Kifiya Expert -> Kifiya Master -> Kifiya Legend

The games

5 games, in the order we should build them

We had 16 ideas. We put them into 5 games. Number 1 should be built first. Each game says why it is in that place.

1

Collector Journey

More good work

Every good task gives points. Points move you up 6 levels, from Kifiya Starter to Kifiya Legend. Each level gives you something better.

Why first: all the other games need points and levels to work. It is also the safest game. Points cost us nothing, and they only come from good work.

What is inside

  • Points and levels: higher levels see better-paying tasks first.
  • Trust Score: a score that shows how good your work is.
  • Badges: small prizes you collect, like "First Task" or "100 Good Tasks".
  • Welcome mission: a short practice task for new people that teaches the app and gives a welcome gift.
  • Later: skill tests that open special tasks, and using good work history to help people get small loans from Kifiya.

Already used by

  • Google Maps Local GuidesPeople add photos and facts about places, earn points, and move up levels with badges.
  • Uber ProDrivers move up tiers by doing good trips and get real rewards at each tier.
  • Stack Overflow reputationGood answers earn points. More points give more rights on the site.
  • Field AgentA quality score decides who sees jobs. Rejected work lowers it. Same idea as our Trust Score.
  • TolokaSkill scores open better-paying tasks. Same idea as our skill tests.
2

Daily Habit

People come back daily

Small goals every day, a count of days in a row, and a weekly gift box.

Why second: it is the fastest way to make people open the app every day, and it is easy to build. It uses the points from game 1, so it comes right after.

What is inside

  • Days in a row: do one good task a day. The longer your count, the bigger the reward. If you miss a day, a "shield" can save your count.
  • Daily goals: a few small goals each day, like "finish your tasks". A bigger goal each week pays a little ETB.
  • Gift box: finish the weekly goal and open a box. There is always a prize inside, like points or airtime. You can never buy a box.

Already used by

  • Duolingo streaksA count of days in a row that makes people come back every day.
  • Duolingo streak freezeA shield that saves your streak when you miss a day.
3

Equb Squads

More people joinInvite friends

Like an equb. A few friends make a team. The team has one goal for the week. If they reach it, everyone gets a bonus.

Why third: this is the best way to bring in new people. Everyone already knows how an equb works. Friends push each other to work. It needs games 1 and 2 first so we can count the team's progress.

How it works

  • A collector at the Kifiya Explorer level makes a squad and invites friends. Others can ask to join.
  • Each Monday the app sets one goal for the squad, based on team size, past work and tasks nearby. The leader cannot change it.
  • Every approved task from any member fills the squad's progress bar.
  • Goal reached by Sunday: every member gets the same bonus. Nobody pays money in.
  • Kifiya sets the rules and the money limit in the admin website.

What is inside

  • Teams: a shared weekly goal and a team bonus.
  • Invite rewards in steps: you get paid in steps as your friend does more good tasks. So you invite people who really work.
  • Monthly card: a nice picture of your month (tasks, ETB earned, level) to share on Telegram or TikTok, with your invite code on it.

Already used by

  • Duolingo Friends QuestsTwo friends share one weekly goal and win a reward together.
  • Strava group challengesFriends make a group, set a goal, and push each other to reach it.
  • Field Agent and PremiseBoth pay collectors for inviting friends.
4

City Conquest

More tasks doneHappy companies

The app shows a map of the city, cut into small pieces like floor tiles. Each piece is about one neighborhood block. The game sends collectors to the places where companies still need information.

What the colors mean

  • Grey piece: no tasks have been done here yet.
  • Gold piece: a company needs information here and nobody has gone yet. Tasks here pay double.
  • Your color: you did the most good tasks here this month, so the piece shows your name or your team's name. Others can take it back by doing more good work there.

Example

  • Kifiya wants to offer small loans to shop owners in Kolfe. It needs each shop's location, a photo, and whether it accepts mobile payments. Nobody has gone there yet.
  • The Kolfe pieces on the map turn gold.
  • Collector-A sees gold pieces 2 km away. She visits 5 shops there. Each task pays 80 ETB, not 40.
  • Those pieces now show Collector-A's name.
  • Kifiya sees on its own map that Kolfe is now covered, and can start offering loans there.

Also part of this game

  • Urgent tasks: a company needs something fast, like Kifiya asking for "20 shops checked in Merkato in the next 2 hours". Collectors nearby get a phone alert. The first ones to finish get double pay.
  • For companies: pay a little more to make a task go faster, run their own named challenge, and see a map of where they still need information.
Why fourth: companies will love it, because collectors go where information is missing, not only to easy places near home. But it is the hardest game to build. Today the phone tells us where the collector is, and we just trust it. Before we pay double for gold pieces, our system must check the location itself, so nobody can fake it.

Already used by

  • PremisePays people to do location tasks like taking photos of shops. Close to Platform-X.
  • Uber surge mapA colored map shows drivers where they are needed and where pay is higher.
  • Pokémon GO gymsTeams claim real places on the map, and others can take them back.
  • Field AgentPays a little more to do several tasks at one place.
5

Leagues and Seasons

People stay long

Each week you play against a small group of people near your level in your city. The best move up, the last move down. Each new season brings holiday events.

Why fifth: a friendly contest keeps the best collectors for a long time. But it needs many active people in each city to be fair. So it works best after the first games bring more people in.

What is inside

  • Weekly leagues: the best players win ETB. We count good work, not just fast work. Only nicknames are shown.
  • Seasons: special events for Enkutatash, Meskel, Genna and Timket, with special badges and double-point days.

Already used by

  • Duolingo LeaguesWeekly groups of learners. The best move up, the last move down.
  • How Duolingo Leagues workA detailed look at why leagues keep people active.

Staying safe

Rules to protect money and trust

A money limitThe game has a fixed ETB budget each month. When it runs out, people get points only.
Watch the costWe check how much ETB the game spends for each active collector.
No buying luckNobody can pay to play. Every gift box has a prize. So it is not betting.
Stop cheatingA daily points limit, one account per phone, location checks, and invite rewards only after real work.
Fair for people with no internetWork counts on the day it was done, not the day it was sent.
PrivacyLists show nicknames. People can hide from public lists.

How we build it

One new part, nothing old breaks

We add one new part to the system that keeps the points and the games. It listens to what already happens in the app, like "task approved". When a cash prize is earned, it asks the wallet to pay it. The way tasks work today does not change.

In the phone app

  • A new "Progress" page: level, days in a row, goals, badges.
  • Today's goals on the home screen.
  • A small celebration when points come in.

In the admin websites

  • Kifiya team: set goals, seasons, prizes and the money limit.
  • Companies: make a task go faster, and see the coverage map.

Extra value for Kifiya

Find the best people to hire

When Kifiya needs to hire, the game data shows who the best collectors are. No guessing from a CV alone.

  • Trust Score shows who does careful, honest work over a long time.
  • Levels and days in a row show who is steady and keeps showing up.
  • Squad leaders show who can lead a team and help others.
  • Skill tests show who is good at special work, like photos or surveys.
  • The city map shows who is ready to travel to new areas.
