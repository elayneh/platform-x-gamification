Platform-X

Collector Growth Plan

Four simple ideas to keep collectors coming back and doing good, approved work.

How Platform-X works today

A quick picture of the app

Companies need information. For example, Kifiya in Addis Ababa wants to know which small shops accept mobile payments. It posts a task. People called collectors do the task on their phone. A checker looks at the answers. Good answers are paid in ETB. Collectors take the money out with Telebirr, Chapa or CBE.

1Company posts a taskIt sets the questions, the pay and the area.
2Collector does itOn the phone, even with no internet.
3Checker approvesGood work is accepted. Bad work is sent back.
4Collector gets paidETB goes into the app wallet.
5Collector cashes outTo Telebirr, Chapa or CBE.

The app already has a wallet, bonuses, invite codes, reminders and tools to run weekly jobs. This plan uses them. We do not change how tasks work.

The problem

Why Platform-X needs this

A task app only works if collectors keep coming back and do good work.

  • People try the app once and leave. A clear profile and levels show new collectors how they can grow.
  • No task, no visit. Weekly goals give a steady reason to come back.
  • Pay alone does not keep people. Levels with real work benefits make collectors want to stay.
  • Invites bring the wrong people. Invite rewards come only after the friend does approved work.
  • Some places get no data. Tasks pay more where data is missing.
  • Careless work hurts quality. Every reward depends on approved work and the Trust Score.

What we want

What we want to grow

  • More people join
  • Collectors keep working week after week
  • Good collectors invite good people
  • More good tasks get done
  • Find the best people to hire

The one rule

Every reward comes from good work

Companies pay us for true, good information. This plan helps that work. It is not a game added on top.

  • Rewards come only when work is approved.
  • Each collector has a Trust Score. Good work makes it go up. Bad work makes it go down.

Two words to know

Trust Score and levels

Trust Score

A score for how good your work is.

Levels

Work grades, like at a job. A good Trust Score and steady approved work move you up.

Kifiya Starter -> Kifiya Verified -> Kifiya Expert -> Kifiya Senior

The plan

4 ideas, in the order we should build them

Each one helps the main job: good, approved data work. Number 1 should be built first.

1

Collector Profile and Trust Score

More good work

Each collector gets a work profile: level, Trust Score, approved tasks and skills. Good work moves you to a higher level, and each level brings real work benefits.

What is inside

  • Work profile: level, Trust Score, approved tasks and skills in one place.
  • Level benefits: better-paying tasks first, more task types and faster cash-out.
  • Welcome task: a short practice task that teaches new collectors how to do good work.

Already used by

  • Uber ProDrivers move up levels by doing good work and get real benefits at each level.
  • Google Maps Local GuidesPeople who add facts about places move up levels.
  • TolokaSkill scores open better-paying tasks.
2

Weekly Goals and Steady Work Bonus

Steady work

Each collector gets a clear weekly work goal. Collectors who meet their goal week after week earn a steady work bonus.

How it works

  • Each Monday the app sets a weekly goal, based on your past work and the tasks near you.
  • Only approved tasks count toward the goal.
  • Meet the goal several weeks in a row to earn a steady work bonus.
  • One free miss: missing one week does not take away your progress.
  • A reminder when new tasks are posted near you.

Already used by

  • BoltDrivers get a bonus for finishing a set number of trips in a week.
3

Invite and Team Program

More people joinGood invites

Collectors invite people they trust. Rewards come only when the new person does real, approved work. Small teams can also share a weekly goal.

How it works

  • Invite rewards in steps: you are paid as your friend completes more approved tasks.
  • Optional small teams: a few collectors share one weekly goal, set by the app. If they reach it, each member gets the same bonus.
  • Nobody pays money in. All bonuses are paid by Platform-X.
  • Kifiya sets the rules and the money limit in the admin website.

Already used by

  • Field AgentPays collectors for inviting friends.
4

Extra Pay for Missing Areas

More tasks doneHappy companies

A map shows where data is still needed. Tasks in those areas pay more, so collectors go where companies need them most.

Example

  • Kifiya needs data on small shops in Kolfe for a loan product. Nobody has gone there yet.
  • The app marks Kolfe as an area that needs data, with higher pay.
  • A nearby collector sees it and visits the shops. Each task there pays more than usual.
  • Kifiya sees on its map that Kolfe now has data.

Also part of this

  • Urgent tasks: when a company needs data fast, nearby collectors get a message and higher pay.
  • For companies: add extra pay to make a task go faster, and see a map of where data is still missing.

Already used by

  • PremisePays people to do location tasks like taking photos of shops.

Staying safe

Rules to protect money and trust

A money limitBonuses have a set ETB amount each month. We also check how much bonus we pay for each approved task.
Stop cheatingBonus limits, one account per phone, location checks, and invite rewards only after real work.
Fair for people with no internetWork counts on the day it was done, not the day it was sent.
PrivacyA collector's profile is seen only by the collector and Kifiya. No public lists.

How we build it

One new part, nothing old breaks

We add one new part to the system that keeps the Trust Score, levels, goals and bonuses.

In the phone app

  • A "My Work" page: level, Trust Score, weekly goal and skills.
  • This week's goal on the home screen.
  • A clear message when a bonus is paid.

In the admin websites

  • Kifiya team: set goals, bonus amounts and the money limit.
  • Companies: add extra pay to a task, and see the map of missing data.

Extra value for Kifiya

Find the best people to hire

When Kifiya needs to hire, the work data shows who the best collectors are. No guessing from a CV alone.

  • Trust Score shows who does careful, honest work over a long time.
  • Levels and weekly goals show who is steady and keeps showing up.
  • Team work shows who works well with others.
  • Skill tests show who is good at special work, like photos or surveys.
  • Work in new areas shows who is ready to travel to new areas.
